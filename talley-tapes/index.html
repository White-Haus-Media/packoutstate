Teaser Untold: The Talley Tapes · Premiering Friday, Oct 30

Before bottle service, before sections, there was Talley. Before the posts, before the stories, before nights became content. When flyers moved hand to hand and word of mouth was currency, Talley became a legendary party known round the state, with students traveling from their respective campuses to be part of it.

Talley was not just a building; it was a rite of passage. If you knew, you went. If you missed it, you heard about it. Nights became lore, passed from generation to generation, sparking debate on who did it best.

No matter the era, the nostalgia takes over, and one phrase emerges:

“Damn… I remember
them Talley parties.”
Everybody who was there

Homecoming Friday night, The Talley Tapes brings the stories to life with a house party experience at House of Art, in the heart of downtown Raleigh.

A premiere night for the people who lived it, the people who heard about it, and everybody ready to be part of the next chapter.
