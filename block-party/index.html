Tailgate, watch party, day party turned night party, all in one place, all on one wristband. The day splits either side of the game. Come when it fits your Homecoming.

Heads up

The game time hasn’t been announced yet. Once it drops, the day below shifts to fit it. The lot may open earlier, and the tailgate window gets longer or shorter depending on kickoff. We’ll make a final announcement as soon as the game time is released.

2:30 PMThe lot opens

Questions

FAQ

What does admission include?

Your ticket gets you one wristband with in-and-out access all day and night. Go back to your hotel, freshen up, and pull back up when you're ready. We recommend purchasing tickets in advance. Capacity is limited and may sell out.

Where should I park?

The best option is the N West St. parking deck, just steps from Killjoy. We also encourage Uber or Lyft for convenience and safety.

Can I bring my own stuff?

Yes, you can bring a chair. No coolers, no outside food or drink. This is a rain-or-shine event.

Will there be food?

Yes. BBQn4U will be onsite serving fried chicken, fried fish, and all the fixins’.

Who's providing the music?

The energy will be set by DJ Fredo, Brint City, and DJ JMar, guaranteed to keep the vibes right all day and night.

Is there security?

Yes. Security will be on site. Bags are allowed but will be searched upon entry.

Could it sell out?

Yes. Once we hit capacity, tickets will no longer be available. Secure yours early.

What's the occasion?

We're celebrating NC State Homecoming, and the Pack Out State Block Party is the place to be all day long.
